Report an application

What to include

  • The application's name as the consent page showed it.
  • Its client ID — it appears in the address bar as client_id.
  • Where you were sent afterwards, and when.

What happens next

The operator reviews the report and may suspend the application, which stops every sign-in to it at once, and contact its developer. If you approved a sign-in you now regret, revoke the application at your account. Security problems in this service itself: see security.
