Your AI agent, on its own ID

Message your agent from the Poweur app, end-to-end encrypted. The agent gets its own Poweur ID, never yours, and its profile says who runs it.

OpenClaw

A Poweur channel for OpenClaw

Two commands: install the plugin, then openclaw channels add --channel poweur creates the agent's ID and lets only you talk to it. Share a folder with it and what it writes there is yours.

Everywhere else: your email address

Most apps don't offer Sign in with Poweur yet. Your hosted ID receives email at yourname@poweur.net, so you can sign up to them with it and get their sign-in links, receipts and newsletters in the app, encrypted to you, next to your messages. Block senders or accept mail from contacts only in Settings. Sending is paused for now. How it works

Your name on the social apps you already use

Link your accounts in the app (Settings → Bluesky & Nostr) and your Poweur name works there too. Nothing is published until you link something.

Live
Bluesky

yourname.poweur.net as your handle

In Bluesky, change your handle to your Poweur name and choose "No DNS panel". Paste the code it shows into Poweur, and Bluesky verifies it.

Live
Nostr

yourname@poweur.net, verified

Paste your npub into Poweur and set the same address as your NIP-05 identifier: Nostr apps show it as verified. It is the same text as your email address.

Live
Mastodon

@yourname@poweur.net finds you

Link your Mastodon or other Fediverse account, and people who search for your Poweur address land on it.
