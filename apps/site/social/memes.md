# Social copy & meme drafts (not on the site)

## "Always has been" (astronauts on the moon)

- Front astronaut, looking at Earth covered in app logos: **"Wait… it's all just ID, messaging and files?"**
- Astronaut behind, holding the gun: **"Always has been."**

Variant for devs: Earth labelled "Slack, Drive, Login with Google, WhatsApp, Stripe webhooks…".

## ID, message and file walk into a bar…

> An ID, a message and a file walk into a bar.
> Bartender: "Can I see some identification?"
> ID: "alice.poweur.net. Here's my signature."
> Message: "I'm with her. End-to-end."
> File: "Put it on her tab — she shared me with you."
> Bartender: "Wait… so you three are an app now?"
> All three: "Always have been."

Shorter one-liner:
> An ID, a message and a file walk into a bar. They leave with a pizza, a payment proof and zero servers.
