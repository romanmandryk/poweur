# Introducing Poweur ID: your name, your inbox, your files

Most apps ask you to create another account, rebuild your contacts and move your data into their system. I'm building Poweur to give those things a common, open foundation.

A **Poweur ID** is a readable name, such as `alice.poweur.net` or a name on your own domain, backed by cryptographic keys you control. It brings together three things:

- **Identity:** prove control of your ID and sign in to apps configured to accept Poweur.
- **Messaging:** exchange signed, end-to-end encrypted messages with people on other Poweur relays.
- **Shared data:** keep files in your own home and grant access to other identities.

You can use hosted services or run your own relay. A hosted name depends on its domain operator; using your own domain gives you more control.

The ambition extends to agents. People and software should be able to work on shared files, with clear permissions, using the tools they prefer. Open formats make that work useful beyond any single app.

Poweur is open source and pre-1.0. The core identity, messaging, sharing and sign-in features exist; the broader agent and app ecosystem is still ahead.

I want an internet where your name, relationships and work remain yours as the tools around them change.
