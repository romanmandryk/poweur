P mark and the logo lockups in `design/` and `brand/` are not licensed under the AGPL or the
Apache License.

You're welcome to:

- say your software "works with Poweur", "supports Poweur ID" or "Sign in with Poweur";
- use the logo on a "Sign in with Poweur" button or to link to poweur.org / poweur.net;
- run your own relay or fork of the code, and describe it truthfully as based on Poweur.

Please don't:

- name a fork, a hosted service or a product "Poweur", or use the logo as its own logo;
- suggest that a relay, app or service is run or endorsed by the Poweur project when it isn't.

Ask if you are unsure: the contact details are on [poweur.org](https://poweur.org/legal/).
